THE PERSON BEHIND BYKIRA

I care about how a site
feels, not just how it works.

I like the point where design and code meet: where something can be technically solid, easy to use, and still have a bit of personality.

I’m the person behind ByKira. I handle the design, development and the small decisions in between, so you are not passed from one person to another or trying to keep several people on the same page.

I have a soft spot for good typography, tidy layouts, subtle motion and interfaces that feel obvious without feeling boring. Most of the work is in getting those details to sit together without overcomplicating the site.
