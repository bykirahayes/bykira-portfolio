Kira aims to make this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology. This statement was reviewed on 29 August 2026.

What you should be able to do

  • Navigate the site using a keyboard.
  • Skip directly to the main content.
  • Zoom text and page content without losing essential information.
  • Use the site with common screen readers.
  • Use forms with visible labels and clear error cues.
  • Experience reduced animation when your device requests reduced motion.

Standard and limitations

The aim is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. Accessibility has been considered in the structure and behaviour of the site, but no check can prove complete accessibility for every person or combination of technology. Decorative visual effects do not carry essential information, and meaningful content is provided as text.

Feedback and alternative formats

If you find a problem, need information in another format, or cannot use part of the site, email use the protected enquiry form. Include the page and what you were trying to do. Kira will aim to reply within 5 working days.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is considered when content and features change. This statement will be updated after material changes or further testing.
