LEGAL / PRIVACY

Privacy,
plainly.

This notice explains what happens to personal information when you visit this website or contact Kira about a project. Last updated 25 August 2026.

Who is responsible

Kira is the controller of personal information handled through this website and project enquiries. Kira is an independent website developer based in Athens, Greece, working with clients internationally.

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email info@bykira.co.uk.

Information used and why

Project enquiries

If you choose to email, the information you include—such as your name, email address, organisation, budget, timing and project details—is used to reply, discuss your requirements and take steps you request before entering a contract. If a project proceeds, relevant correspondence may also be used to perform the contract, keep business records and establish or defend legal claims.

Ordinary email

Email is handled by the email providers used by you and Kira. Please do not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and agreed in advance.

Website technical data

The hosting provider may process limited technical information, such as IP address, browser details, requested page, date and time, to deliver and secure the site. This is based on legitimate interests in providing a reliable and secure website.

Sharing, transfers and retention

Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate email, host the website or deliver agreed work, or where disclosure is required by law. Those providers may process information outside the UK. Where UK data-protection law applies, appropriate safeguards are used where required.

Unsuccessful enquiry correspondence is normally kept for up to 12 months. Client and transaction records may be kept for up to 7 years after the relationship ends where needed for tax, accounting, contractual or legal purposes. Information may be deleted sooner when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access to your information, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office if UK data-protection law applies, or to your local data-protection authority. Visit ico.org.uk for UK complaint guidance.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not currently set cookies, use analytics, embed advertising trackers or use browser storage. It also uses local system fonts, so loading a page does not contact an external font provider. If this changes, this notice and any consent controls will be updated before non-essential technology is used.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the website or working practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
