Questions with clear answers

01What do you build, and do you handle both design and development?+

I design and develop marketing sites, portfolios, studio websites, landing pages, and focused digital experiences. Visual direction, responsive layouts, interaction, accessibility, and the final build are handled as one connected process. Every site is tested across mobile, tablet, and desktop.

02How long will it take, and what will it cost?+

Most projects take four to eight weeks, although a focused landing page may be quicker and a larger custom site may take longer. Pricing depends on scope, content, complexity, and timing. After our first conversation, I provide a clear fixed proposal covering the work, schedule, payment stages, and boundaries.

03What do you need from me, and can you help with content?+

I need an understanding of your goals, audience, and any existing brand or content materials. You provide the knowledge about your work; I help turn it into a clear structure, identify gaps, and refine the website copy. You do not need to arrive with every answer—shaping the idea is part of the process.

04Can I update the site, and what happens after launch?+

When editing is part of the brief, I can connect a suitable content system and provide a straightforward handover. At launch, I complete final checks and help with the domain and hosting. Ongoing maintenance, support, or future improvements can be arranged separately.

05How do we get started?+

Send me a short explanation of your idea, what the website needs to achieve, and any timing you have in mind. I will reply with the next useful questions and we can arrange a conversation.
