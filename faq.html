01What kind of websites do you work on?+

Landing pages, business websites, portfolios and more custom builds. If the project needs something more specific, I scope it around the actual requirements instead of forcing it into a fixed package.

02How much does a website cost?+

Landing pages usually sit around £50–£150, business or portfolio sites around £150–£300, and custom builds start from £500. The final price is agreed once the scope is clear.

See services & pricing ↗
03Do I need all of the content ready first?+

No. Rough copy, notes, existing material or even just the key points are enough to start. I can help shape the structure and make the content fit the website properly.

04How long does a project usually take?+

A landing page is usually around 2–3 weeks, a larger business site around 4–6 weeks, and custom work depends on the scope. I confirm the timeline before work starts.

05When do I pay?+

Only once the scope, price and expectations are agreed. You will know exactly what is being built before payment is requested.

06What happens after the site goes live?+

I handle the final checks and handover, and I can include editing access or a content system where that makes sense. If you need help after launch, that can be discussed as part of the project too.

07Do you handle both design and development?+

Yes. The structure, visual direction, responsive design, interactions and final build are handled as one connected process, so there is no designer-to-developer handoff.

08Can you work with an existing website?+

Yes. That might mean improving what is already there, rebuilding it more cleanly, or using the current site as a starting point for something new.
